#### User inputs

<dl>
  <dt>Initial value: $\mathbb{R}_{\geqslant0}$</dt>
  <dd>The initial value of the concentration</dd>
  <dt>Rate granularity: `Mass conserving | Unbalanced | Per place`</dt>
  <dd>Should a single rate be prescribed per flow (mass conserving), or should separate consumption and production rates be given; in the latter case, should they be given per flow (unbalanced) or per input/output place (per place)</dd>
  <dt>Rate: $\mathbb{R}_{\geqslant0}$</dt>
  <dd>*(Only if **RateGranularity** = Mass conserving)* The rate coefficient ($r$) of the reaction</dd>
  <dt>Consumption: $\mathbb{R}_{\geqslant0}$</dt>
  <dd>The consumption rate coefficient ($\kappa$), either per flow or per (input/output) place, depending on **RateGranularity**</dd>
  <dt>Production: $\mathbb{R}_{\geqslant0}$</dt>
  <dd>The production rate coefficient ($\rho$), either per flow or per (input/output) place, depending on **RateGranularity**</dd>
  <dt>Duration: $\mathbb{R}_{\geqslant0}$</dt>
  <dd>The total duration of the simulation in units of time</dd>
</dl>
